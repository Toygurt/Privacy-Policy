Privacy Policy

LittleBall Run: 3D Ball Runner · LB Games
Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play LittleBall Run: 3D Ball Runner (the “Game”, Android package ball.runner), published on Google Play by LB Games (“we”, “us”).

Summary. We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information ourselves. There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device. The Game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect limited device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The Game does not require an account or sign-in.

Your game progress and settings (best runs, points, unlocked skins, sound and vibration options) are saved only on your device. We never receive a copy of them. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show, limit, measure and protect ads, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID,
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (not precise) location,
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, language),
  • interactions with ads (for example views and taps), and diagnostic information about ad performance and crashes.

This information is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Data sent by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit.

3. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the Game, Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice about personalised ads. You can change it later with the privacy options button in the Game’s Options screen.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Security & privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.

4. In-app purchases

If the Game offers an optional purchase (for example to remove ads), payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details.

5. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep a database of users. Information stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google’s policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are handled directly by Google; we are happy to help you with any question.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
